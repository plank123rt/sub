4. Rahu and Ketu: conjunction, aspects and why

KP gives the nodes no sign, so they act as agents of the planet conjunct with them, the planet aspecting them, their star lord and their sign owner, in that order of strength. Aspects are measured by degree (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition), with a 3°20′ orb. Vedic sign-to-sign aspects are not used, so your habit of ignoring an aspect until the degree is close is the KP method.
