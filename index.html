Dasha · Bhukti Activation

For a Mahadasha (MD) lord, the houses it signifies come from its own chain (self + star lord + sub-lord + any conjunct planet — see cards above). During each Antardasha (AD) inside that Mahadasha, the AD lord's own chain becomes active too, and results for the period come from the combination of both sets of houses. A house lit up by both MD and AD lords (red) is the strongest for that period; a house touched by only one of them (gold = MD only, blue = AD only) is weaker; a grey/blank cell means neither lord's chain reaches that house in this Antardasha, so it stays quiet during that period. Sequence follows the fixed Vimshottari order (Ke→Ve→Su→Mo→Ma→Ra→Ju→Sa→Me→back to Ke), starting from the Mahadasha lord itself. No timing/duration is given here, only which houses turn on.
