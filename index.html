Dasha · Bhukti Activation

For a Mahadasha (MD) lord, the houses it signifies come from its own chain (self + star lord + sub-lord + any conjunct planet). During each Antardasha (AD) inside that Mahadasha, the AD lord's own chain becomes active too, and results for the period come from the combination of both sets of houses. Sequence follows the fixed Vimshottari order (Ke, Ve, Su, Mo, Ma, Ra, Ju, Sa, Me, back to Ke), starting from the Mahadasha lord itself. No timing or duration is given here, only which houses turn on.

Overall Theme Comparison - Rahu Mahadasha vs. Mars Mahadasha

The big-picture pattern, independent of which Antardasha is running
Rahu Mahadasha

Wealth, property, status & foreign linkage

Rahu itself carries 2, 4, 8, 10, 11, 12 as strong houses. Since Rahu is the Mahadasha lord throughout, this baseline pull toward money, property, career status, sudden gains, and cross-border matters is present in every Antardasha of the cycle, it never fully disappears. Some Antardashas amplify it heavily (Rahu-Jupiter, Rahu-Saturn), some soften it (Rahu-Mars), but the underlying current holds.

Structurally weak for the whole Mahadasha: marriage / partnership. Rahu does not touch the 7th house at all, so relationship progress only surfaces when the Antardasha lord happens to supply it (Venus, Moon, Mercury, Mars) - outside those four sub-periods, partnership matters tend to stall.

Mars Mahadasha

Self-drive, competition, speculation & relationships

Mars itself only carries 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 as strong houses, notably including the 7th. So the baseline pull toward partnership and relationship activity is present in every Antardasha here, because Mars is always reinforcing it.

Structurally weak for the whole Mahadasha: wealth, property, career status, and foreign matters - Mars's own signification barely touches 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, or 12. Those themes swing hard depending on the Antardasha lord: nearly silent in Mars-Mars, Mars-Ketu, Mars-Sun; strongly activated in Mars-Rahu, Mars-Jupiter, Mars-Saturn, and especially Mars-Mercury.

In one line: Rahu Mahadasha is the more financially/materially consistent era, with relationships as the wildcard. Mars Mahadasha is the more relationally/personally consistent era, with wealth and career as the wildcard.

Rahu & Mars Mahadasha - Antardasha Theme Analysis

What becomes active, sub-period by sub-period, based on combined house signification
Method. For each Mahadasha–Antardasha pair, the house list above for each planet is combined. Houses signified by both planets (shown in gold) are the strongest and most reliably activated during that sub-period; houses signified by only one planet are secondary support. That combined house list is then read against the standard KP event groupings established earlier: 7 / 2-11 marriage, 10 / 2-6-11 career & business, 11 / 2-6-10 funding & gains, 11 / 3-9-12 foreign investment, 2 / 6-11 wealth, 2-11 (with 5-9-10 support, avoiding heavy 8/12) millionaire-tier wealth, 5 / 2-11 childbirth, 1 / 11 good health, 1 / 6-8-12 disease or health crisis, 3-9-12 foreign travel, 3-4-9-12 foreign settlement, 4 / 11-12 property, and 3-6-9-11 litigation/contracts. This is a signification-based reading only - it does not account for planetary strength, retrogression, combustion, aspects, or the actual cuspal sub-lord chain of a real chart, so treat it as a thematic map of what is on the table during each sub-period, not a certainty.
Rahu Mahadasha
Rahu's own signification: 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12 (strong) · 3, 9 (weak) · 7th untouched. Antardashas in standard Vimshottari order: Rahu → Jupiter → Saturn → Mercury → Ketu → Venus → Sun → Moon → Mars.
Rahu → Rahu
Peak Rahu signature
Combined (self only): 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12. 7th remains completely untouched.
  • Wealth accumulation (2, 11)
  • Foreign / cross-border dealings (4, 12)
  • Speculative or unconventional income (5)
  • Status / career shift (10)
  • Sudden gains, inheritance (8)
  • Marriage / partnership - unsupported
This is Rahu operating at full, undiluted strength, so it is the clearest single window for Rahu's own signature results: unconventional or outsized gains, foreign-linked money, property or import/export activity, and a general willingness to break from convention to get ahead. The 2-11 overlap makes this a genuinely strong wealth-and-gain period, and 4+12 together lean toward foreign investment, foreign property, or expenditure abroad. Caution: 6-8-12 are all active simultaneously, which is the classical health/legal-stress combination, so physical strain, disputes, or financial leakage can run alongside the gains. The 7th house (marriage, partnerships, direct business dealings) gets zero support from Rahu itself here, so relationship or partnership matters tend to stall or feel one-sided in this sub-period unless another running period is compensating.
Rahu → Jupiter
Strongest career/wealth window in this Mahadasha
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Jupiter: 1,2,4,6,8,10,11 (weak 3,5 · untouched 7,9,12). Overlap: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11 - seven of nine houses reinforced by both planets.
  • Career advancement / promotion (2,6,10,11)
  • Property acquisition (4)
  • Financial windfall / inheritance (8)
  • Business growth & funding (2,6,10,11)
  • Foreign travel - only weakly present (9 weak on both)
  • Marriage - untouched by Jupiter, weak overall
This is arguably the strongest all-round period in the entire Rahu Mahadasha for the career-and-money group. The near-total overlap on 1-2-4-6-8-10-11 stacks almost the entire "job/business/wealth" house group (2-6-10-11) on top of self-effort (1), property (4), and sudden gain (8). This reads as a genuine promotion, business-expansion, or funding-round window, and a good period to pursue a millionaire-tier wealth push, since 2 and 11 are both doubly reinforced. Interestingly, despite Jupiter's natural karakatva for the 9th house (luck, higher learning, long journeys), it is not structurally reinforced in this chart's chain, so foreign/higher-education themes stay muted here even though the wealth themes are strong.
Rahu → Saturn
Slow, structural wealth & property builder
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Saturn: 2,3,4,5,6,8,10,11 (untouched 1,7,9,12). Overlap: 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11.
  • Long-term wealth building (2,11)
  • Property / real estate (4)
  • Speculative gains tied to children or creativity (5)
  • Sudden gain or inheritance (8)
  • Career consolidation (6,10)
  • Self-image, partnership, foreign - all weak or untouched
Seven houses overlap here, and the flavor is distinctly Saturnine: slower, more effortful, but structurally sound. This period favors accumulating assets rather than a single dramatic windfall, real estate purchases, methodical career consolidation, and steady growth of savings/investments (2-11). The 5th house being doubly reinforced also opens a genuine speculative or investment-return theme, not just childbirth. Because both planets leave 1, 7, 9, and 12 weak or untouched, this is not a strong period for identity-level reinvention, marriage, or foreign relocation, the gains here are mostly domestic and asset-based.
Rahu → Mercury
Contracts, partnerships & foreign-linked business
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Mercury: 1,3,5,6,7,8,9,11,12 (untouched 2,4,10). Overlap: 1, 5, 6, 8, 11, 12; Mercury alone brings in 3, 7, 9.
  • Foreign investment / funding (11,3,9,12)
  • Business partnerships & contracts (7)
  • Foreign travel / higher education abroad (9)
  • Sudden gains (8)
  • Short-distance movement, negotiation (3)
  • Direct property purchase (4) - untouched by Mercury
Mercury is the antardasha that finally activates the 3-9-12 foreign-travel group together with Rahu's own 12th, plus it brings the 7th house (partnership, marriage, direct dealing with others) into play, something Rahu alone in this chart cannot do. This makes it a strong window for foreign-linked business deals, cross-border funding or investment, contract signings, and partnership formation, whether that's a co-founder, a spouse, or a formal business tie-up. It is one of the better antardashas in this Mahadasha for the specific "foreign investment / funding from abroad" theme, since it stacks 3, 9, 11, and 12 together.
Rahu → Ketu
Transitional / karmic-axis period
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Ketu: 1,3,5,6,7,11 (weak 2,10 · untouched 4,8,9,12). Overlap: 1, 5, 6, 11; Ketu adds 3, 7 that Rahu doesn't have.
  • Fulfillment of a specific goal (11)
  • Self-driven effort, competitive success (1,6)
  • Short travel, relationship activity (3,7)
  • Property, foreign settlement, big windfalls - mostly untouched
Running Rahu-Ketu together (the two nodes) is classically read as a transitional or closure-oriented stretch rather than a clean expansion period. The overlap is narrower than the other Rahu antardashas, and neither planet reinforces property (4), sudden windfalls (8), higher learning/foreign luck (9), or foreign settlement (12) here, so this tends to be a period of finishing or resolving existing matters (fulfillment of a specific desire via 11, competitive or self-effort wins via 1/6) rather than opening major new fronts in wealth, property, or foreign expansion. Partnership and short-distance movement (3,7, via Ketu) do get some activation.
Rahu → Venus
Best marriage / foreign-luxury window in this Mahadasha
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Venus: 1,3,5,6,7,8,9,12 (untouched 2,4,10,11). Overlap: 1, 5, 6, 8, 12; Venus alone strongly brings 7, 9, 3.
  • Marriage / romantic partnership (7)
  • Foreign travel, luck, luxury (9)
  • Foreign settlement / expenditure abroad (12)
  • Speculative or creative gains (5)
  • Inheritance or sudden gain (8)
  • Pure career/status growth (10) - untouched by Venus
Venus is the antardasha within Rahu Mahadasha most worth flagging for relationship questions: it is the only Rahu sub-period where the 7th house gets real, direct support (from Venus, Venus's natural signification for marriage adds further weight). Combined with Rahu's own 9-adjacent and 12th-house strength, this period also favors foreign travel, luxury acquisition, or settling abroad, and the 5-8 overlap adds a romantic/speculative-gain flavor. If marriage or a significant relationship commitment is "due" in this Mahadasha, this is the most natural antardasha for it to land in. Pure career or status elevation (10) is comparatively quiet here, since Venus doesn't reinforce it.
Rahu → Sun
Authority, status & property, less foreign/gains
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Sun: 1,2,4,6,8,10 (weak 3,5 · untouched 7,9,11,12). Overlap: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
  • Status, recognition, authority (10)
  • Property (4)
  • Family wealth / accumulated assets (2)
  • Sudden gain (8)
  • Gains / fulfillment of desire (11) - untouched by Sun
  • Foreign matters (9,12) - untouched by Sun
A strong six-house overlap gives this period real weight for status, authority, and property, this suits dealings with officials, government, or senior figures, and property matters tied to status (buying a home that reflects position, for instance). But because Sun leaves 11 and 12 untouched despite Rahu being strong there, this period tends to under-deliver on the "big gain" and "foreign" fronts relative to other antardashas in this Mahadasha, the wins here are more about position and asset value than liquid windfalls. The 6-8 overlap also means health or legal friction is worth watching.
Rahu → Moon
Strongest foreign-travel/settlement window in this Mahadasha
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Moon: 1,3,6,7,8,9,10,11,12 (untouched 2,4,5). Overlap: 1, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12; Moon adds 3, 7, 9 strongly.
  • Foreign travel & settlement (3,9,12 - full group)
  • Marriage / partnership (7)
  • Career & public recognition (10)
  • Gains / fulfillment (11)
  • Sudden events (8)
  • Direct property purchase (4) - untouched
This is the single antardasha across the whole Rahu Mahadasha where the complete 3-9-12 foreign-travel-and-settlement group is activated together, since Moon brings 3 and 9 while Rahu supplies 12, and both reinforce it further through the overlap on 1, 6, 8, 10, 11. If foreign relocation, an overseas career move, or a long-distance journey is going to happen in this Mahadasha, this antardasha is the strongest structural candidate for it. It also carries real partnership (7) and career (10) weight, so a foreign opportunity tied to work or marriage is a plausible combined reading. Property purchase in the ordinary domestic sense (4) is not supported here, if property comes up, it likely relates to a foreign or transitional living situation rather than a settled home purchase.
Rahu → Mars
Conflict, competition & effort, not a big-wealth window
Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 · Mars: 1,3,5,6,7 (untouched 2,4,8,9,10,11,12). Overlap: 1, 5, 6; Mars adds 3, 7 that Rahu barely or doesn't touch.
  • Competitive drive, disputes, litigation (6)
  • Courage-driven short travel (3)
  • Relationship or business friction (7)
  • Property, career elevation, big wealth, foreign - all unsupported by Mars
This is the weakest antardasha in the Rahu Mahadasha for the wealth, property, career, and foreign themes that dominate the rest of the period, Mars simply doesn't reinforce Rahu's houses 2, 4, 8, 10, 11, or 12. What it does bring is friction: competitive pressure, potential disputes or legal matters (6), assertive short-distance activity (3), and a partnership house (7) that is active but through Mars's more combative signature rather than Venus's harmonious one, so relationship or business-partner conflict is more likely than romantic progress here. Health and accident-caution is also worth flagging given Mars's natural karakatva combined with 6th-house activation. Best read as a period to manage disputes and stay disciplined rather than to expect major financial breakthroughs.
Mars Mahadasha
Mars's own signification: 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 (strong) · 2 weak · 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 untouched. Antardashas in standard Vimshottari order: Mars → Rahu → Jupiter → Saturn → Mercury → Ketu → Venus → Sun → Moon.
Mars → Mars
Personal drive, narrow scope
Combined (self only): 1, 3, 5, 6, 7. Houses 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 all untouched.
  • Self-assertion, initiative (1)
  • Short travel, courage, communication (3)
  • Romance, speculation, children (5)
  • Competition, disputes (6)
  • Partnership / marriage, open conflict (7)
  • Wealth, property, career elevation, foreign - all untouched
Mars at full, undiluted strength is a personally active but structurally narrow period. It is genuinely useful for initiating things: starting a relationship or business partnership (7), pursuing a competitive goal (6), or making a courage-driven move (3, 1), and it carries real romantic/speculative energy (5). But because 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 are all untouched by Mars alone, this is not a period where major financial windfalls, property deals, career promotions, or foreign opportunities are structurally supported, whatever happens on those fronts will be riding on other running periods (Bhukti/Antara), not on Mars-Mars itself.
Mars → Rahu
Major scope expansion - wealth, property, foreign gains open up
Mars: 1,3,5,6,7 · Rahu: 1,2,4,5,6,8,10,11,12 (weak 3,9 · untouched 7). Overlap: 1, 5, 6; Rahu alone adds 2, 4, 8, 10, 11, 12 that Mars-Mars completely lacked.
  • Wealth, property, career elevation - newly activated (2,4,10,11)
  • Sudden or unconventional gains (8)
  • Foreign-linked matters, though weaker (12, weak 9)
  • Assertive, unconventional partnership dynamics (7, Mars side only)
  • Partnership lacks Rahu's reinforcement (7 untouched by Rahu)
This antardasha is the clearest upgrade in the entire Mars Mahadasha for wealth, property, and career, Rahu brings in the whole set of houses (2,4,8,10,11,12) that pure Mars-Mars could not touch. Expect the possibility of an aggressive but genuine leap: unconventional income, a property deal, or a career jump, layered on top of Mars's own courage and competitive drive. One nuance worth noting: the 7th house is strong through Mars but completely untouched by Rahu, so any partnership or relationship activity in this period is likely to carry an assertive, unconventional, or one-sided quality rather than a smooth, mutually-supported one.
